# Shin Wellness Creative Direction

## Creative concept

The central idea was **“a softer way to come back to yourself.”**

Shin Wellness was treated as a warm, personal wellbeing brand rather than a clinical service, luxury spa, or high-performance coaching business. The design aims to feel like opening a quiet journal, receiving a thoughtful note, or stepping into a sunlit room.

The emotional qualities were:

- Calm without becoming sterile
- Premium without feeling exclusive
- Personal without relying on fabricated intimacy
- Playful without becoming childish
- Encouraging without making health promises
- Spacious, slow, and gently optimistic

The generated inspiration string was used privately. Its repeated pairs, occasional clusters, and irregular rhythm led to:

- Two primary session cards
- Three core upcoming-program cards
- Repeated botanical symbols
- Alternation between dense content groups and open space
- A mix of orderly grids and slightly imperfect decorative elements

The string itself never appears in the design.

## Color direction

The palette is muted, botanical, and warm:

| Color | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Warm ivory | `#F7F3EA` | Main background and light buttons |
| Deep moss | `#34483F` | Primary text, buttons, premium session card, newsletter |
| Muted lilac | `#DED5EB` | Emotional softness, cards, notes, and decorative accents |
| Soft peach | `#E9B89F` | Warmth, sun shapes, and human energy |
| Sage tones | Various | Hills, leaves, secondary backgrounds, and gentle contrast |

The site avoids pure white and black. This keeps contrast softer while maintaining readable text.

The dark moss sections give the page visual weight at important conversion moments:

- The seven-day session package
- The final email signup section

## Typography

Two type families create the brand voice:

- **Lora** for headlines and emotional phrases
- **DM Sans** for navigation, body copy, pricing details, buttons, and labels

Lora makes the page feel editorial, thoughtful, and human. Italics emphasize emotional phrases such as:

- “yourself again”
- “a little reset”
- “free for you”
- “come back to you”
- “more wellbeing”

DM Sans gives the practical information a clean, approachable tone.

Typography follows a clear hierarchy:

- Large editorial hero statement
- Medium section headlines
- Strong but restrained product titles
- Uppercase, letter-spaced eyebrow labels
- Readable supporting copy
- Pill-shaped metadata and “coming soon” labels

The revised desktop scale uses:

- 16–18px supporting text
- 15px navigation and interactive controls
- 11–12px metadata
- A container that expands to 1,400px

## Layout direction

The page uses an editorial landing-page structure with strong horizontal alignment and generous vertical rhythm.

The main content progression is:

1. Brand introduction
2. Primary paid sessions
3. Free resources
4. Upcoming paid programs
5. Retreats and recommendations
6. Final email signup

The hero uses an asymmetric two-column composition:

- Large message and calls to action on the left
- Botanical arch illustration on the right
- Small floating decorations to break the grid

A narrow values strip follows the hero and repeats three brand beliefs:

- Less doing. More being.
- Care that meets you where you are.
- Your pace is a good pace.

Offer sections use paired and three-column arrangements derived from the string’s repeated patterns.

The primary paid offers are deliberately larger and visually stronger than the future products. This preserves the immediate business goal: booking sessions.

## Hero art direction

The hero artwork is a custom SVG illustration rather than a stock photograph.

It combines:

- An architectural arch
- Layered green hills
- A peach sun
- A dark botanical stem
- Soft background foliage
- A winding ivory path
- Small lilac and peach dots
- A slightly rotated lavender note

The note reads:

> less pressure.  
> more presence.

The overall composition suggests growth, grounding, sunlight, and returning to a steady path.

A subtle paper-grain treatment gives the otherwise clean SVG a tactile, printed quality.

## Brand symbols

A recurring sprout symbol acts as the brand’s main visual motif. It appears in:

- The favicon
- Session cards
- Email signup areas
- Resource illustrations
- Recommendation cards
- Dialogs

Small sunbursts, stars, dots, and cross-shaped marks appear throughout the page. They create rhythm and make the layout feel handmade without becoming decorative clutter.

The Shin wordmark uses:

- Lowercase serif lettering
- A small star-like symbol
- Widely spaced uppercase “WELLNESS”
- A restrained editorial identity rather than a conventional fitness logo

## Paid session presentation

The two session options are presented as a matched pair.

### Personal reset

- Pale sage card
- Dark moss purchase button
- Positioned as the gentle entry point
- Lets the client choose one of the seven session focuses

### Seven-day pack

- Full moss background
- Ivory purchase button
- Higher visual weight
- Presented as continued daily support
- Includes all seven focuses once, with Shin tailoring their order to the individual

The seven session focuses are:

1. Neck and shoulders
2. Chest opening
3. Lower back (lumbar)
4. Hamstrings
5. Core strength
6. Insomnia
7. Fatigue

The focus descriptions use supportive, non-guaranteed language. They communicate intended comfort, flexibility, balance, relaxation, energy, and clarity without presenting medical treatment or promised outcomes.

Pricing is oversized and uses serif typography so it feels intentional and transparent.

Benefits are short, plain-language statements. The personal reset uses subtle checkmarks, while the seven-day pack uses a compact numbered session list. The cards avoid exaggerated promises, testimonials, urgency, countdowns, or aggressive sales language.

## Free resources

The free resources use more playful art direction.

### Book recommendations

The book recommendations card includes:

- Three layered book covers
- Sage, lilac, and peach tones
- Botanical mark
- Hand-lettered editorial title
- Soft sage background
- A small floating star

It evokes a thoughtfully kept bedside reading stack and quiet moments of reflection.

### Wallpapers

The wallpaper card includes:

- Two overlapping phone-sized cards
- Lilac and peach background tones
- Small encouragement phrases
- Botanical illustrations
- Slight rotation and imperfect stacking

This section adds personality while remaining within the calm brand system.

## Upcoming offerings

The three primary upcoming programs are shown in a clean three-card grid:

1. Emotional regulation PDF
2. Four-week check-in program
3. CBT-informed habit coaching

Each has:

- A numbered position
- A soft circular icon
- A category and price label
- A short editorial heading
- Concise supporting copy
- An interest-specific email action

The cards are intentionally quieter than the session cards. They communicate that these offerings are growing without competing with the current purchase goal.

## Retreats and recommendations

These concepts appear as wider editorial preview cards.

The retreat card uses a small abstract landscape with:

- An arch
- Layered green hills
- Peach sun

The recommendations card uses a tilted lilac note with a sprout and the phrase:

> little good things.

These are previews rather than full navigation destinations, keeping the first launch focused.

## Copy direction

The writing uses small, reassuring phrases and avoids clinical language.

Recurring language includes:

- A little
- Soft
- Space
- Pause
- Reset
- Grow
- Come back to you
- At your own pace

The voice is:

- Gentle
- Direct
- Non-judgmental
- Brief
- Emotionally aware
- Free from wellness jargon

The site does not claim credentials, guaranteed outcomes, transformations, or testimonials that have not been provided.

## Conversion direction

The page has two conversion paths.

### Primary conversion

Purchase a session:

- “Find your reset”
- “Book a session”
- “Buy a single session”
- “Buy the 7-day pack”

The checkout buttons open external HTTPS payment or booking pages.

### Secondary conversion

Join the email list:

- General footer signup
- Offering-specific signup dialogs
- Interest tracking for each future product

Visitors can express interest in a particular offering without losing the context of what they selected.

If checkout or Google Sheets is not configured, the design gives an honest unavailable message rather than pretending an action succeeded.

## Interaction direction

Interactions are restrained:

- Pill buttons rise slightly on hover
- Navigation links reveal a subtle underline
- Hero elements enter with a small upward fade
- Dialogs use a blurred moss overlay
- Buttons include simple directional arrows
- Smooth scrolling connects page navigation to sections

Animations are disabled automatically when the visitor has reduced-motion enabled.

The signup dialogs:

- Open over the current page
- Explain the selected offering
- Keep keyboard focus inside
- Close with Escape or the close control
- Return focus to the original button
- Preserve the email if submission fails

## Responsive direction

The mobile layout keeps the same visual identity while changing the content hierarchy:

- Navigation links collapse away
- The booking button remains visible
- Hero copy comes before the illustration
- Session and resource cards stack vertically
- Upcoming products become a single-column list
- The newsletter form stacks
- Footer content becomes a compact grid

Desktop layouts expand up to 1,400px so the site feels appropriately sized on large and Retina displays.

The full desktop treatment now begins at 901px, covering common Mac browser window sizes whose screenshots may be exported at twice their CSS resolution.

The design was verified at:

- 320px
- 390px
- 768px
- 901px
- 1,000px
- 1,100px
- 1,280px
- 1,440px
- 1,920px

## Overall visual reference

The resulting direction sits between:

- An independent wellness journal
- A modern botanical editorial
- A personal handwritten note
- A quiet lifestyle brand
- A thoughtfully designed coaching studio

The central design principle is that every part of the page should feel like an invitation rather than a demand.
