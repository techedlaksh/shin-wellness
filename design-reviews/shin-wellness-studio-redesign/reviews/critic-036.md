Biggest gaps:

1. **The lilac upcoming panel interrupts the page without resolving the composition.** Its rightward placement leaves a large, inert ivory pocket on the left, while its contents feel compressed toward the bottom. Make this a shorter, more deliberately positioned marginal note with a clearer internal hierarchy.
2. **Supporting typography is consistently too small and faint.** Descriptions, metadata, captions, and button labels lose presence against the large serif headlines. Increase the smallest text and its contrast; premium quietness should come from spacing, not diminished legibility.
3. **The pricing spread is visually forceful but internally unbalanced.** The angled divider feels arbitrary, and the pale “Inside one day” insert competes with the pack’s title and price. Use a more intentional shared alignment across both offers and subordinate the insert so the purchase decision reads immediately.
4. **The hero illustration stops just short of bespoke artistry.** Repetitive leaf hatching, uniformly outlined petals, and the detached peach disk feel like assembled decorative ingredients. Give the botanical a more distinctive silhouette, varied line pressure, and a stronger relationship to the headline.
5. **The free-resource section is too evenly merchandised.** Two centered mockups followed by similarly structured text blocks resemble a conventional product grid. Introduce meaningful asymmetry through scale, crop, and placement so the books and phone notes feel discovered on a desk.
6. **Softness is repeated until it loses emphasis.** The reassurance strip, abundant small captions, and recurring “little,” “soft,” and “come back” phrasing make every area carry the same emotional register. Remove redundant microcopy and let a few specific, confident statements anchor the page.

Highest-leverage move: Recompose the entire free-and-upcoming area as one asymmetric editorial arrangement, turning the oversized lilac block into a compact marginal note and eliminating the dead space beneath the resource grid.
