Score: 7.8/10

Aesthetic read: Warm, restrained, and convincingly gentle; the palette and serif typography already feel cohesive. The free-resource collage comes closest to the intimacy of a quiet journal, while the rest feels like an elegant wellness landing-page template. A studio would bring more distinctive drawing, stronger reading hierarchy, and greater compositional confidence.

Biggest gaps:
1. **The paid offers have less personality than the free resources.** The book and wallpaper collage receives the richest art direction; the sessions get large prices and rectangular panels. Give the paid section an equally memorable editorial gesture that makes the experience tangible while preserving its stronger commercial hierarchy.
2. **Supporting text is consistently too small and faint.** Navigation, descriptions, labels, and session details become visual texture instead of useful reading. Increase their size and contrast, then remove redundant microcopy to preserve spaciousness.
3. **The hero botanical feels assembled from familiar wellness ingredients.** A peach circle, thin looping lines, and a generic flower communicate the category without establishing Shin’s identity. Commission a more characteristic illustration with expressive line variation, surprising cropping, and a deliberate relationship to the headline.
4. **The handmade devices accumulate into a formula.** Tilted papers, notebook rules, sprouts, italic emphasis, and overlapping cards repeatedly signal “gentle.” Establish one signature paper treatment and reserve the other devices for a few meaningful accents.
5. **The upcoming-offers composition feels unresolved.** The broad lilac note sits above a narrow, partially concealed strip, creating an awkward attachment rather than a satisfying grouping. Recompose these as one clearly subordinate editorial module with an intentional edge alignment and visible hierarchy.
6. **The page lacks a second substantial visual pause.** After the expansive hero, most sections return to headings beside compact information blocks. Introduce one spacious, experiential composition within the paid-session area so the page has a memorable middle.

Highest-leverage move: Rebuild the paid-session section around one distinctive, generously scaled editorial composition that makes receiving care feel as tangible and desirable as the free-resource artwork.
