Biggest gaps:
1. **The hero lacks an unmistakable signature.** Its left-aligned headline and large flower illustration are familiar wellness ingredients; develop a more idiosyncratic botanical composition with expressive cropping, irregular linework, and a stronger relationship to the typography.
2. **Secondary text becomes visual dust.** Descriptions, labels, pricing qualifiers, and footer details are consistently tiny and faint; enlarge essential copy, strengthen contrast, and remove low-value microcopy so the quietness comes from space.

4. **The free section commands disproportionate attention.** Its enormous book illustration and separate wallpaper collage rival the paid offering; compress the free resources into a smaller editorial interlude and reserve the richest visual storytelling for the sessions.
5. **The lower half settles into a predictable catalogue.** The upcoming offers become a stack of pale rectangular cells with similar text density; replace that inventory rhythm with one featured idea and a quieter, visibly subordinate collection.
6. **The package card dominates through sheer dark area.** The floating “7,” rotation, oversized price, and wide button accumulate competing emphases; retain the moss field but simplify its hierarchy and give the single-session card a more confident typographic presence.

Highest-leverage move: Rebuild the hero around one unmistakably authored botanical-and-type composition that establishes a visual grammar the rest of the page can inherit.
