Score: 7.7/10

Aesthetic read: The palette, literary typography, and generous spacing establish a convincing sense of quiet care. It feels polished and coherent, but still closer to a tasteful wellness template than a distinctive studio identity. The strongest handmade moments belong to the free gifts, while the paid experience feels comparatively institutional.

Biggest gaps:

1. **The paid sessions lack the visual intimacy given to the free offers.** Their large, square pricing panels resemble subscription software, while the book and wallpaper mockups have texture and personality. Bring a restrained paper treatment or bespoke editorial composition into the paid section, making the experience—not just the price—its focal point.
2. **Secondary typography becomes an almost unreadable gray texture.** Navigation, session details, explanatory copy, and footer text are consistently tiny and faint. Increase their size and contrast, then remove redundant copy so legibility does not compromise spaciousness.
3. **The hero botanical looks assembled from familiar wellness illustration ingredients.** The peach circle, pale green blob, isolated flower, dotted curve, and decorative swoosh collectively feel stock-adjacent. Commission one more expressive botanical gesture with distinctive line quality and fewer disconnected accessories.
4. **The page repeats its editorial formula too predictably.** Oversized serif headings, a softened contrasting phrase, uppercase eyebrow, tiny explanatory paragraph, and thin rules recur across sections. Give one major section a different compositional rhythm—a personal note, an oversized single statement, or an intimate illustrated vignette.
5. **The upcoming-offers section becomes a miniature product directory.** Five compact offers, repeated availability labels, and multiple small links create administrative density after the more generous free-gifts section. Reduce this to one compelling preview and a quieter collective invitation.
6. **The handmade effects vary in visual credibility.** Flat botanical linework sits beside floating merchandise mockups with conspicuous soft shadows and rotation. Establish one consistent material language: believable paper, restrained shadows, and irregularity that feels observed rather than applied.

Highest-leverage move: Redesign the paid-session section as the page’s most distinctive and inviting editorial moment, giving it the material warmth and visual storytelling currently reserved for the free gifts.
