2. Aesthetic read: Warm, composed, and unusually coherent: the oversized botanical, restrained palette, and asymmetric session spread communicate a considered wellness brand. The page feels premium and approachable, but its repeated editorial formulas make the lower half more predictable than the opening. The handmade character is strongest in the hero and becomes increasingly decorative afterward.

3. Biggest gaps:

   1. **The lower page repeats the same composition too often.** Free resources, upcoming products, and newsletter all use a large serif statement on the left with smaller content on the right; give one section a radically different scale and arrangement to restore surprise.
   2. **The hero’s flower is expressive, but its surrounding arc, peach disc, curved baseline, and italic caption compete as separate decorative ideas.** Refine these into one intentional illustration composition with a clearer relationship to the headline.
   3. **Utility text is consistently too small and faint.** Navigation, offer qualifiers, supporting copy, and footer details become visual texture; enlarge the smallest type and strengthen contrast so the softness comes from spacing and color rather than reduced legibility.
   4. **The free-resource mockups feel familiar and commercially staged.** Floating booklets and rounded phone cards with generic affirmations dilute the journal-like personality; show more distinctive actual content through a cropped page, an annotated excerpt, or a singular illustrated wallpaper.
   5. **The lilac upcoming panel carries disproportionate visual weight.** Its large colored rectangle and multiple headings make unfinished offers feel like a second product launch; compress it into a smaller, more intimate studio note so paid sessions retain greater dominance.
   6. **Hairline rules and micro-labels have become a default organizing device.** Nearly every module receives dividers, uppercase labels, or numbered details; remove a substantial share and let typography, proximity, and occasional asymmetry establish the hierarchy.

4. Highest-leverage move: Recompose the entire free-and-upcoming area as one distinctive editorial interlude, replacing the repeated two-column product presentation with a generous, irregular arrangement of real pages and one compact studio note.
