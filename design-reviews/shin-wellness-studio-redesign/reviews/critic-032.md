Aesthetic read: Warm, restrained, and recognizably editorial; the moss, ivory, botanical drawing, and paper objects establish a convincing quiet-journal mood. The paid offer has appropriate prominence, and the page avoids the usual wellness stock-photo clichés. It still feels more like an exceptionally polished template than a studio with a distinctive visual point of view.

Biggest gaps:
1. **The oversized flower dominates without enough artistic richness.** Its thin, uneven stem and repetitive leaf hatching feel underresolved at this scale; commission a more expressive botanical composition with deliberate variations in line, texture, and silhouette.
2. **The composition becomes predictable below the hero.** Full-width color bands, left-aligned headings, two-column offers, and hairline dividers repeat; introduce one consequential editorial interruption—an overlapping note, asymmetric inset, or unusually cropped illustration—to change the page’s rhythm.
3. **Supporting typography is too faint and diminutive.** Descriptions, card labels, prices’ qualifiers, and button text recede into near-microtype; increase their size and contrast while preserving the generous spacing.
4. **The main offer pair contains conspicuous empty territory.** The personal-session card’s button sits far below its short benefits list, while the pack relies on an enormous ghosted “7”; tighten the cards and let the seven-day path become a more substantive, beautifully drawn organizing element.
5. **The lilac upcoming-offers panel feels like a separate component library.** Its rectangular enclosure, evenly packed text, and isolated color shift lose the tactile character of the free-resource illustrations; give it the physical logic of a journal insert, with more intentional margins and a single distinctive handmade detail.
6. **Gentleness is repeated until it becomes a mannerism.** “Little,” “soft,” “slow,” “thoughtful,” and “come back” recur across headings and captions; retain a few signature phrases and make the remaining copy more specific to what each offering actually provides.

Highest-leverage move: Recompose the paid-session section as a distinctive editorial spread, using a richer seven-day drawing, stronger supporting typography, and asymmetric spacing to establish a visual identity beyond the hero.
