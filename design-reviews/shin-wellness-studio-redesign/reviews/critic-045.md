Aesthetic read: The ivory, moss, and editorial typography create a convincing sense of quiet care, and the paid pack has appropriate prominence. But the page feels more like a polished wellness template than a distinctive studio identity: the handmade gestures decorate an otherwise very predictable composition.

Biggest gaps:
1. **The page settles into stacked horizontal bands.** After the expressive hero, nearly everything becomes a heading, supporting copy, columns, and rules; introduce one substantial asymmetrical composition that carries the journal aesthetic into the middle of the page.
2. **The hero illustration lacks the refinement its scale demands.** The enormous thin stem, loosely scratched leaves, peach circle, and faint arcs read as assembled decorative ingredients; commission a cohesive botanical drawing with more intentional silhouette, line quality, and negative space.
3. **Too much supporting typography is whisper-small.** Descriptions, labels, prices’ qualifiers, and button text lose presence against large serif headlines; increase their size and contrast, and reduce the number of competing micro-labels.
4. **The seven-day pack feels like a conventional pricing card wearing a paper note.** The rigid dark rectangle and oversized price dominate its character, while the tilted insert feels attached afterward; integrate the note into a more distinctive editorial composition that communicates the experience of seven days.
5. **The free-resource mockups repeat familiar wellness-brand shorthand.** Pastel books, tilted phones, tiny sprouts, and affirmations look interchangeable with countless gentle-living brands; give these objects a specific illustration or typographic language recognizably belonging to Shin.
6. **The final third loses compositional energy.** A peach strip with a lilac rectangle leads into a broad moss newsletter block with a small floating form; make this ending feel like an actual thoughtful letter, with a stronger relationship between text, form, and botanical detail.

Highest-leverage move: Recompose the paid-session section as a distinctive, asymmetrical journal spread, giving the central commercial moment the same expressive ambition as the hero.
