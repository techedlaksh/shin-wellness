Aesthetic read: The palette, expressive serif, and oversized botanical establish a credible, gentle wellness identity. The strongest moments feel editorial; the weaker ones feel like a polished landing-page template decorated with stationery. It needs more compositional conviction and fewer miniature labels to reach a studio-level finish.

Biggest gaps:
1. **The lower page loses its compositional backbone.** Two wide freebie columns abruptly give way to a small, centered lilac rectangle floating in excess space. Integrate the upcoming offer into an intentional asymmetric composition, with a clearer relationship to the material above.
2. **The hero illustration feels diagrammatic.** Thin leaf outlines, repeated hatching, a flat peach disk, and near-geometric arcs produce a constructed botanical rather than a memorable artwork. Commission a more distinctive silhouette with varied stroke weight, irregular pigment, and selective detail.
3. **The paid offers speak different visual languages.** The single session is an open editorial column; the seven-day package is a heavy, shadowed rectangular slab. Build a shared typographic and structural system, then express the package’s priority through proportion and color without the floating-card shadow.
4. **Too many tiny labels dilute the hierarchy.** Eyebrows, numbering, captions, status notices, italic reassurance, and fine rules surround nearly every interaction. Remove secondary framing aggressively and let fewer, more readable pieces of information carry each section.
5. **The free-resource mockups look staged rather than personal.** Both illustrations repeat the familiar tilted-product arrangement, with soft shadows and decorative sprigs. Give these objects distinct treatments—an open book excerpt and a single expressive wallpaper crop would introduce genuine variety.
6. **The type rhythm is overly uniform below the hero.** Most sections settle into a serif heading, muted explanation, tiny uppercase label, and underlined link. Introduce one markedly different editorial moment with larger supporting text, unexpected alignment, and enough restraint around it to feel deliberate.

Highest-leverage move: Recompose the entire free-and-upcoming area as one asymmetric editorial spread, replacing the disconnected lilac card and repeated product displays with a clear, continuous reading sequence.
