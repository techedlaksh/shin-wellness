Score: 8.1/10

Aesthetic read: A convincing, restrained wellness identity with an appealing botanical opening and clear emphasis on paid sessions. The middle and lower page become a catalogue of pale rectangles and miniature annotations, losing the hero’s generosity. It feels carefully art-directed, but still more like a polished template than a distinctive personal journal.

Biggest gaps:
1. **The lower half loses compositional energy.** Free resources, upcoming offers, and the newsletter follow predictable horizontal bands; give one section a radically simpler, oversized editorial composition with deliberate empty space.
2. **Too much information lives at whisper volume.** Navigation, descriptions, offer metadata, and secondary links are visibly tiny and low-contrast; enlarge the supporting typography and remove enough microcopy to preserve breathing room.
3. **The upcoming-offers section feels like an inventory.** One large lilac card sits beside four compressed ruled rows, making several different experiences visually interchangeable; feature one developed proposition and condense the rest into a genuinely secondary announcement.
4. **The hero illustration is distinctive in scale but conventional in execution.** Uniform leaf outlines, translucent blobs, a dotted arc, and scattered decorative marks assemble familiar wellness ingredients; commission a more characterful botanical gesture with irregular line quality and fewer surrounding devices.
5. **The stationery treatment repeats until it becomes a formula.** Ruled paper, tilted rectangles, tiny uppercase labels, and miniature sprouts recur across unrelated offers; reserve the paper-note language for one memorable moment and establish stronger material contrast elsewhere.
6. **The free-resource mockups promise tactility but appear weightless.** Floating pastel books and affirmation cards feel generic and digitally assembled; use fewer, larger objects with more convincing paper edges, shadows, and typography.

Highest-leverage move: Recompose the entire free-and-upcoming stretch around one oversized editorial feature, replacing the sequence of pale cards and compact rows with a stronger rhythm of scale and silence.
