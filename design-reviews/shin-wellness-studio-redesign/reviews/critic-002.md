Aesthetic read: The palette, restrained typography, and paper references make this convincingly calm and welcoming. It feels like a polished wellness template rather than a distinctly authored studio identity: almost everything is equally quiet, equally tidy, and equally precious. The botanical hero supplies warmth, but the page needs stronger moments of contrast and less miniature supporting content.

Biggest gaps:

1. **The visual hierarchy becomes uniformly hushed.** Tiny labels, faint descriptions, hairline rules, and discreet links repeat throughout a very long page. Increase supporting text and interaction sizes, reduce incidental copy, and give the paid-session decision a clearly dominant typographic moment.

2. **The hero illustration uses familiar wellness shorthand.** An arch, peach sun, curved path, and geometrically clean plant create an attractive but interchangeable brand image. Commission a more peculiar botanical composition with irregular drawing, expressive cropping, and a silhouette that belongs specifically to Shin.

3. **Serif-plus-italic emphasis becomes a formula.** “Yourself again,” “a little reset,” “Free for you,” “come back to you,” and “more wellbeing” all deploy essentially the same gesture. Establish fewer, more deliberate italic moments and introduce contrast through scale, line length, and placement.

4. **The paid offering turns into a dense catalogue too quickly.** The two pricing panels have useful prominence, but the attached seven-focus grid creates a compact block of administrative detail. Make the session choice feel generous and immediate, then present the focus areas as a simpler, more breathable secondary layer.

5. **The handmade language remains mechanically contained.** Tilted note illustrations appear inside rigid rectangles; the staggered free-resource cards still read as assembled modules. Let one substantial paper or botanical element cross a section boundary, and vary the relationship between image, text, and empty space.

6. **The upcoming-offers panel asks for too much attention.** Three ruled rows followed by two more cards give speculative offerings an elaborate catalogue treatment. Compress this into a more selective field-note composition with one featured possibility and quieter secondary entries.

Highest-leverage move: Recompose the paid-session section as the page’s unmistakable focal point, using larger readable content, stronger contrast in scale, and substantially less catalogue detail.
