Aesthetic read: Calm, coherent, and convincingly premium, with an attractive moss-and-ivory foundation. The opening feels personal; the lower page increasingly feels like an elegantly styled product catalogue. It has the right ingredients, but the illustration and composition need more individual character.

Biggest gaps:
1. **The botanical hero feels assembled from familiar wellness shapes.** Oversized flat leaves, an arch, a peach disc, and a tilted affirmation note are an overly recognizable combination; commission a more observant, irregular botanical drawing with a distinctive line and fewer decorative components.
2. **The paid offering is an imposing rectangular slab.** Two equal-width cards over a full-width menu create a dense pricing-table silhouette; preserve its prominence while introducing more editorial spacing, a clearer recommended path, and less enclosure.
3. **Free and upcoming offers occupy too much of the visual story.** Two illustrated free offers followed by five upcoming items make the lower page feel like a crowded roadmap; condense upcoming work into a quieter journal entry and let available sessions remain the central proposition.
4. **Supporting typography is excessively small and faint.** Navigation, descriptions, labels, and links repeatedly become visual dust beside generous serif headings; enlarge the useful reading text and establish fewer, stronger type levels.
5. **The staggered free-offer layout feels mechanically offset.** Two adjoining rectangular units form a predictable staircase with stranded whitespace; create a genuinely composed spread using unequal image sizes, a shared baseline, and one restrained overlap.
6. **The “field notes” panel simulates paper through rules rather than material character.** Its enclosing border, margin line, numbered rows, and inner tiles accumulate administrative structure; simplify the grid and introduce one convincing handwritten or lightly textured detail.

Highest-leverage move: Replace the stack of enclosed offer panels with a more selective editorial composition that gives paid sessions a generous central stage and compresses the speculative catalogue.
