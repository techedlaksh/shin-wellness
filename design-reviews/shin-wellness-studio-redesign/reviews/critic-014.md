Aesthetic read: Warm, restrained, and coherent—the journal sensibility comes through, and paid sessions clearly carry the most weight. The editorial typography and moss/ivory palette feel considered, but the page becomes increasingly conventional after the hero. A studio-level version would have fewer decorative gestures and more distinctive composition.

Biggest gaps:

1. **The lower page loses its compositional confidence.** “More ways to come back to you” pairs a large lilac rectangle with a narrow, densely stacked list, leaving awkward empty space below the rectangle. Recompose this as a deliberate editorial spread with stronger alignment, a clearer reading sequence, and less disparity in text scale.
2. **The hero botanical feels like assembled vector primitives.** Flat elliptical leaves, the peach flower, background oval, dotted line, and peach squiggle compete without creating a memorable illustration. Commission one expressive botanical drawing with distinctive line quality; remove supporting marks that dilute it.
3. **The free-resources illustration overstates unfinished offers.** The enormous sage panel and floating book mockup dominate two modest “coming soon” cards. Reduce the mockup’s footprint and build a more intimate arrangement of actual paper objects, with clearer relationships between each object and its offer.
4. **Secondary typography becomes excessively small and faint.** Session details, focus descriptions, upcoming-offer copy, and footer text register as texture before information. Increase their size and contrast, then cut copy where needed to preserve the calm.
5. **The handmade vocabulary becomes a repeated treatment.** Rotated panels, paper textures, tiny sprouts, pastel blocks, and curved lines appear across multiple sections. Reserve imperfection for two or three expressive moments and let the remaining structure become more exact.
6. **The broad horizontal bands make the scroll feel templated.** Hero, reassurance strip, sessions, freebies, upcoming offers, and newsletter each occupy a self-contained slab. Introduce one meaningful overlap or asymmetric transition so the page feels like a composed publication rather than stacked landing-page modules.

Highest-leverage move: Recompose the entire section below the paid sessions as one distinctive editorial sequence, replacing the oversized freebie mockup and cramped upcoming list with a clearer, more spacious hierarchy.
