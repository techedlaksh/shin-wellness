Score: 7.6/10

Aesthetic read: Warm, composed, and convincingly gentle, with strong moss-and-ivory restraint and clear emphasis on paid sessions. It feels like a polished wellness website, but the journal-like intimacy and handmade unpredictability remain mostly decorative. The typography supplies more personality than the composition.

Biggest gaps:
1. **Too many rectangular modules.** Pricing, freebies, upcoming offers, and newsletter settle into familiar horizontal bands and boxes. Introduce one memorable editorial interruption—a generously scaled, slightly irregular paper composition that changes the page’s rhythm.
2. **Supporting text is consistently tiny and faint.** Navigation, offer descriptions, labels, and links retreat beneath large headlines. Increase their size and contrast so the softness comes from spacing and color, rather than reduced legibility.
3. **The hero illustration feels assembled from clean vector components.** The peach circle, arch, winding path, and oversized leaves read as recognizable wellness graphics. Commission a more distinctive botanical gesture with irregular contours, finer detail, and a less diagrammatic arrangement.
4. **The free section gives its book mockup excessive real estate.** The illustration occupies roughly as much width as either offer, while the staggered panels muddy their relationship. Treat the book as an intimate object within a clearly composed two-offer spread.
5. **The upcoming section becomes a miniature product directory.** Its featured strip, divided columns, tiny illustrations, and repeated links compress several different ideas into one busy block. Reduce their prominence and present fewer, more generously spaced previews.
6. **The surface treatment is almost uniformly digital and flat.** Repeated rules, smooth fills, and precise edges overpower the occasional tilted book. Introduce restrained paper grain and a consistent hand-drawn line character, concentrated in a few meaningful places.

Highest-leverage move: Recompose the free-offer section as a distinctive, spacious journal spread that breaks the page’s repeated box-and-band rhythm.
