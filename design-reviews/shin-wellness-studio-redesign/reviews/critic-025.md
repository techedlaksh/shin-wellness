Aesthetic read: Warm, restrained, and recognizably aligned with the brief; the moss pricing panel and overlapping stationery are its strongest moments. It feels like a polished wellness template with tasteful editorial touches, rather than a distinctive studio identity. The softness is convincing, but too much of the page recedes into tiny text, pale surfaces, and familiar botanical symbolism.

Biggest gaps:
1. **The header has no visible brand anchor.** “Skip to content” occupies the upper-left identity position while Shin appears only in the footer. Give the masthead a confident wordmark and make navigation feel deliberately composed.
2. **The hero illustration is generic.** A single outlined flower, peach disc, enclosing arc, and loose wave read as stock wellness ingredients. Develop an unmistakable botanical drawing language with richer irregularity, unexpected cropping, and a stronger relationship to the headline.
3. **The secondary typography is excessively timid.** Navigation, descriptions, captions, and offer labels become pale texture throughout the page. Increase their size and contrast, then reserve delicate typography for genuinely incidental information.
4. **The lower page loses compositional momentum.** “More ways to come back to you” introduces another large heading and spacious field, but resolves into one small lilac card and a disconnected text fragment. Build one purposeful studio-notes composition that feels unfinished by intention, rather than underfilled.
5. **The handmade language repeats predictable devices.** Tilted pastel rectangles, notebook rules, tiny sprouts, and miniature stationery appear as an applied aesthetic kit. Keep fewer motifs and give them more specific material character, stronger scale changes, and less uniformly tidy execution.
6. **The free-offer section attracts disproportionate visual attention.** Its large illustrated objects and overlapping panels are more expressive than the paid sessions. Transfer some of that distinctive art direction to the paid offering and compress the free section into a quieter supporting moment.

Highest-leverage move: Create a proprietary botanical-and-lettering composition for the hero and carry its distinctive visual character into the paid sessions, establishing an identity stronger than the familiar pastel wellness vocabulary.
