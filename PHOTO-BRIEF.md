# Shin Wellness photo brief

The current page needs four final image files across three photographic roles. The existing images are layout placeholders and can be replaced without changing the page structure.

## Priority 1: a real portrait of Shin

Provide two crops from the same portrait sitting if possible.

- `practitioner-wide.png`: landscape, ideally 3:2 and at least 3000 px on the long edge. Place Shin in the left third and leave the right half calm and uncluttered so the website copy remains readable.
- `practitioner-portrait.png`: vertical 4:5, ideally at least 2500 px on the long edge. Keep Shin's face clear, with comfortable room above her head and around her shoulders.

Aim for direct but relaxed eye contact, natural expression, soft morning or evening light, and simple practice clothing. This pair is the clearest trust signal on the page.

## Priority 2: the hero movement image

- `session-active.png`: a vertical action photograph of Shin demonstrating an accessible movement or guiding one participant. The movement, face, arms, and hands should all be legible.
- Also send a looser horizontal alternative from the same moment. The site currently crops this image differently on desktop and mobile, so generous space around the subjects is important.

A strong existing beach-session photograph can work here. Prioritize a close, recognizable teaching moment over a distant silhouette or a scenic beach view.

## Priority 3: how the session feels

- `process-note.png`: a vertical image, ideally 4:5 and at least 2500 px on the long edge. Show Shin observing, explaining, or demonstrating movement with a participant. Her attention and the participant's movement should be easy to understand at a glance.

This is the best second use for the beach archive. It should add evidence of Shin's way of working rather than repeat the hero pose.

## Useful extras

Send one wider beach-session scene and up to two natural details, such as feet on a mat or Shin demonstrating a hand or arm position. They are not required for the current layout, but will be useful for future pages and social posts.

Seven separate photographs—one for each theme—are not needed for this design. The text menu communicates the choices more clearly and keeps the page from feeling like a catalogue.

## Delivery and permissions

- Send original, uncropped, full-resolution files whenever possible; no screenshots, filters, baked-in text, signatures, or watermarks.
- Keep color natural and consistent across the set.
- Confirm permission from every identifiable participant before publication.
- If a beach photo is used while the booking offer remains online, accompany it with the factual context: “From Shin's in-person beach sessions.” This prevents the photograph from implying that the pictured location is what someone is booking.
